2 composite indices that turn dozens of raw urban metrics into single, interpretable scores: a **Green Index** capturing environmental quality and access to green space, and a **Prosperity Index** capturing economic vitality.

> Confidential due to NDA.

## Consistency by construction

The interesting thing isn't any one index; it's that they're built as a *suite*. Both share the same construction discipline: transparent metric selection, normalization that prevents any single unit from dominating, documented weighting, and academic framing of what the score is claimed to represent. Because they share scaffolding, a tract's Green and Prosperity scores are directly comparable, and improvements to the methodology propagate to both rather than being re-implemented twice.

The academic version of the Prosperity Index reached its 15th iteration, each one tightening the methodology, the validation, or the presentation. That iteration count is the real story: a composite index earns trust through repeated, documented refinement, not through a single clever formula.
