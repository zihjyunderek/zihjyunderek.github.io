The City Design Index (CDI) at Gensler Research Institute asks whether the design of a neighborhood is linked to how its residents feel about their city. Each milestone meeting closed a stage of that work with a deck; the archive opens with the 2025 MGWR study of New York City that came before the index. I built them from the analysis I ran, for analysts and for leadership, and from September on in English and Chinese.

> The decks, the survey behind them and every result are confidential under NDA. This page lists what each deck covered. Its one drawing is redrawn from public census boundaries and shows no survey data.

## How the decks are made

- **One headline per slide.** Each title states what the slide shows; the evidence sits under it, the source and caveats at its foot.
- **Numbers from the analysis, not by hand.** Every figure and table is generated from the result files of the run it reports, so a rerun updates the deck instead of a copy.
- **Two languages, one source.** The English and Chinese editions are built from the same content, so they never drift apart.
- **Method decks beside result decks.** Each result deck has a companion that explains the test behind it to analysts, so a finding can be checked before it is presented.
- **A new variable is argued before it is added.** A proposed metric gets its definition, its formula, its coverage in every city, a check against the measure it would replace, and what it adds; only then does it enter the model.
